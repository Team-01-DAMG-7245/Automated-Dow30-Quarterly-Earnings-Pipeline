b The aggregate incremental cost of the personal use of a Company vehicle is determined by (i) calculating the incremental vehicle cost
per mile by dividing the annual lease and fuel costs by the total annual miles; (ii) multiplying the executive’s total 2024 personal miles by
the incremental vehicle cost per mile; and (iii) adding the incremental driver cost (the total 2024 driver hours for the executive’s personal
use multiplied by the driver’s hourly rate).
