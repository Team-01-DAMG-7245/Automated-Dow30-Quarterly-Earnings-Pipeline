Prioritizing accessibility
Honeywell is steadfast in our commitment to creating
an inclusive and accessible world, as highlighted in our
Accessibility Statement. This statement is a testament to
Honeywell's dedication to inclusion and diversity, ensuring that
all individuals can access Honeywell's products, technology,
services and employment opportunities. Honeywell is dedicated
to empowering employees with disabilities by providing
accessible spaces and fostering an environment where everyone
can contribute to shaping the future. To push us forward on our
ongoing universal accessibility journey, Honeywell created a
specialized role solely focused on promoting accessibility. This
role is a crucial step in Honeywell’s journey to create a more
accessible world.
