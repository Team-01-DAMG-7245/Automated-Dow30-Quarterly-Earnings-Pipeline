Domestic Disney+ average monthly revenue per paid subscriber increased from $7.70 to $7.99 due
to increases in pricing, partially offset by a higher mix of subscribers to promotional offerings.
International Disney+ average monthly revenue per paid subscriber increased from $6.78 to $7.19
due to increases in pricing and higher advertising revenue, partially offset by a higher mix of subscribers
to promotional offerings.
Hulu SVOD Only average monthly revenue per paid subscriber was comparable to the prior
sequential quarter as lower advertising revenue was offset by increases in pricing and a higher mix of
subscribers to higher priced multi-product offerings.
Hulu Live TV + SVOD average monthly revenue per paid subscriber increased from $95.82 to $99.22
primarily due to increases in pricing.
