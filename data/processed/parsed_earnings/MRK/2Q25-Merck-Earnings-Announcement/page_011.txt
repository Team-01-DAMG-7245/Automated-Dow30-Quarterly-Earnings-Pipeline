Manufacturing and R&D Investment
The Company continued to make long-term investments in its U.S. manufacturing and
R&D capabilities. This includes the start of construction for a $1.0 billion, 470,000-square-foot
state-of-the-art biologics center of excellence in Wilmington, Delaware, which will serve as a
launch and commercial production facility and the primary U.S. manufacturing site for
KEYTRUDA. In addition, the Company announced an $895 million expansion of its Animal
Health manufacturing facility in De Soto, Kansas; the 200,000-square-foot facility will increase
capacity for Animal Health vaccines and biologic products.
