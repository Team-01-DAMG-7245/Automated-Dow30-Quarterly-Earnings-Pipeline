Transition assistance
Transition assistance programs may be provided to support separated employees as part of a
workforce restructuring. Such benefits are subject to local plans, laws and country guidelines,
but may include the following:
• Severance benefits, which may include severance pay based on an employee’s level and years
of service
• Outplacement job transition assistance
• Health and wellness benefits for a defined time period
