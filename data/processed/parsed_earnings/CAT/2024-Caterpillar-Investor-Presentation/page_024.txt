and believe the non-GAAP measures will provide investors with useful perspective on underlying business results and trends and aid with assessing
our period-over-period results. In addition, we provide a calculation of ME&T free cash flow as we believe it is an important measure for investors
to determine the cash generation available for financing activities including debt repayments, dividends and share repurchases.
