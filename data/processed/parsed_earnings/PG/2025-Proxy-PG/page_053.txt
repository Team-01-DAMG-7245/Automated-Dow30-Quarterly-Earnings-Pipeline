LONG-TERM INCENTIVE PROGRAM GRANT
The LTIP grant is the second component of the Company’s long-term incentive compensation for its senior
executives. Executives can elect to receive all or a portion of their LTIP grants in either RSUs or stock options prior
to the grant date, with the exception of the CEO, whose grant form and amount is solely determined by the C&LD
Committee. Stock options do not vest (and therefore are not exercisable) until three years from the date of grant,
and they expire ten years from the date of grant or earlier in the case of certain termination events. RSUs cliff-vest
three years after grant date and are delivered, upon vesting, in shares of common stock, along with compounded
dividend equivalents. These awards focus executives on the long-term success of the Company.
