COMPENSATION DISCUSSION & ANALYSIS
Compensation Pay Mix
We design our programs so that the main components of NEO compensation vary by type (fixed versus
performance-based), length of performance period (short-term versus long-term), and form (cash versus equity).
These compensation components are determined by the performance of the individual, the performance of the
individual’s business unit, and the performance of the Company as a whole. The mix of components is designed to
incentivize both individual accountability and collaboration to build long-term shareholder value. The charts
below show the mix of the four main components of pay and the mix of fixed versus at-risk pay.
